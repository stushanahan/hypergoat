Our Story

Two Cousins. One Vision.

Two cousins from Ballymun and Sheriff Street keeping Dublin caffeinated since June 2023. Darren brought the barista experience, Johno brought the operations know-how — so they decided to team up and make it happen.

After searching for the right location and facing a few setbacks along the way, the first Hyper Goat finally opened on Thomas Street. Darren ran the shop full-time while the behind-the-scenes work kept everything moving.

From day one, the goal has been simple: great coffee, great people, and building something special together. What started with just two people has grown into a team of eight, and with the fourth location coming soon, the team's expected to grow to around fifteen.

June 2023
The first Hyper Goat opens on Thomas Street, Dublin 8. Just Darren behind the bar and a dream to build something real.
April 2024
Second location opens at Wellington Quay — bringing the dark-mode energy to the banks of the Liffey.
April 2025
Third store opens in Swords. The brand goes north Dublin. The team grows to eight.
Coming Soon
